# Volume N: what the pages have set (FIRST to LAST)

*Lives at `story/volume/<volume>-digest.md`.*

*The page record, by chapter, for drafters and critics, built from the prose only; the prose wins where they differ. A small agent appends one block per chapter as each batch closes and brings **Standing** up to date; the supervisor checks every quote with `grep -F`. Agents read this, and the chapter before closely, in place of the volume's earlier chapters ([the reading list](../workflows/drafting/drafter.md#the-reading-list)).*

## Standing

As of the end of LAST.

- **<Each person on the page so far>**: what the reader knows of them now: names in use, where they live, what they carry, the habits shown, what they know and do not.
- **Where they live now**: one line.
- **Objects**: each object in play, and where it is.
- **Places**: the places shown, with the parts the reader has seen.
- **Terms**: the setting's words in use on the page, as the page spells them.
- **Routines shown in full**: each routine the volume shows step by step (a journey, a trade, a counter, a camp), with the chapters that showed it whole and those that cut it to a line. From its third showing, a routine gets a line or two, and that showing's own event is kept whole (the procedures critic reads this line).
- **Refrains, full showings kept**: each refrain the writer has kept, and where it is shown whole; between those, a gesture or a word (the habits critic reads this line, with `tools/refrains.py`).
- **Open asks**: each request, promise or question someone put on the page that a later page should answer: who asked, where, and whether it is answered yet (the continuity critic reads this line).

## FIRST <Chapter title>

- **When and where**: the day or season; the places.
- **Who**: who appears, with what the page gives of each for the first time.
- **Done**: what happens, in order, one clause each.
- **Objects**: what is given, taken, found or lost.
- **Said**: the lines a later page may echo or answer, quoted.
- **Known**: who learns what; who hides what from whom.
- **Planted**: what a later page may pay off, quoted, without its meaning.
