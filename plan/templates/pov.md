# POV file: [the book, or chapter NNNNN]

*Lives at `story/voice/pov.md` or `story/voice/pov-NNNNN.md`.*

*`story/voice/pov.md` sets the narration for the whole book; `story/voice/pov-NNNNN.md` sets it for one chapter that differs (a letter, an interlude, another narrator, a chapter with two heads). The drafter and every critic read it, and where it and the voice sheet differ, this file wins. Without it, every critic judges every chapter against the main narrator's rules, and any other narration arrives buried in false findings. Fill in each line; delete the guidance.*

- **Person and tense:** [first person / close third / omniscient third; past / present.]
- **Time stance:** [told as it happens (no hindsight), or told from after (how much hindsight, and never what).]
- **Whose head:** [one narrator throughout; or one head per section, named in each section's first sentence, each change marked by a section break; or, for omniscient, who may be entered and who never is.]
- **Distance:** [how close: the narrator's attention only; free indirect thought allowed or not; how wide an omniscient narrator ranges, and where (a drafter left to choose stays close to the main character).]
- **May know:** [for each head: what they know at this point and where they learned it. A head that believes wrong things, or a narrator meant to be unreliable, gets a knowledge guard, `story/voice/knowledge/<head>.md` ([the template](knowledge.md)): what they know, believe wrongly and do not know, chapter by chapter. Link it here.]
- **The narrator never lies:** what the narration states as fact is true in the book's world. It may hold a thing back, give a belief as a person's own, or leave the reader to puzzle; it never states a falsehood, or a half truth meant to mislead. A head's wrong belief is given as theirs, never as the world's fact. [Keep this line; add any stance of your own, e.g. an unreliable narrator, with what the reader is given to see through it.]
- **Must not know, or never tells:** [the held facts for this stretch, by link to the reader key's "must not get" lines; for an omniscient narrator, what it chooses never to tell.]
- **Witness reach:** [scenes or beats this narrator cannot see, and how they reach the page instead (heard, reported, missed); any clue those beats carry, and how it still reaches the reader.]
- **Register, per head:** [link to each head's voice sheet; for a head with none, three lines: diction, rhythm, what they notice and what bores them.]
- **Samples, per head:** [the writer's own pages for each head; for a head with none, provisional samples the writer has approved, so the measures mean something.]
- **Measures:** [the tells.py range from each head's samples, or "none set" until samples exist.]
